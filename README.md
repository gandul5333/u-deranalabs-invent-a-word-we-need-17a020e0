## The word

> **cancelief** (n.) The secret relief you feel when plans are cancelled and your evening is suddenly free again.

**cancelief** — *cancel* + *relief* — names the quiet, slightly guilty rush of joy when a plan falls through and your evening is handed back to you. English has words for disappointment when plans collapse, but none for the relief so many of us actually feel.
